## Custom HTML certificates

You can now fully customize learner certificates using HTML and CSS. Whether you want to replicate your company’s brand identity, add QR codes, dynamic backgrounds, or include unique formatting — it’s all possible.

![](/images/webflow/688c4083d30286b0187aaa3f-685d5ddb7e9035d8bf7fb035-html.svg)

## Academy AI Agent

We just launched our AI Agent! Now you can build a complete academy from scratch—structure, content, and all—in minutes. Just tell it what you need, and it gets to work. No templates, no drag-and-drop—just magic.

![](/images/webflow/688c40996e94156bbfbb3e35-681b3ba91e4080c134ff0acd-group-2058.svg)

## Custom private and public pages

Qurioos now supports both public and private content pages under the same domain. This means you can host landing pages, resource hubs, and logged-in academy content all in one seamless experience—improving SEO, user trust, and consistency across your brand.

![](/images/webflow/688c40c8c014fd68867641e6-680c5b767c49075223aa9bad-67e661d378c23e9003ce1be1-group-2017.svg)

## Add certification to Linkedin

Users who complete your courses can now add their certification to LinkedIn in just one click. It boosts your credibility as an academy, gives users a sense of accomplishment, and turns every certificate into a mini marketing channel for your brand.

![](/images/webflow/688c41c683f8490edbf01541-680c5eecfee124cd0ee23d6f-67e664fc32f32eda36c60ff9-group-2018.svg)

## Improved security and monitoring

We added Google reCAPTCHA v3, stronger IP-blocking rules, and upgraded spam monitoring. This protects your academy from fake signups, bot activity, and misuse—so you can focus on real users, not noise. Our team also keeps an eye on suspicious activity so you don’t have to.

![](/images/webflow/688c41d283f8490edbf01867-680c5f170770563fdb1dc762-67e667b42b24bc78f45da708-group-2020.svg)

## Custom email domain and templates

You can now send emails from your own domain with your logo and colors to match your brand. This means clearer communication with your users and a more polished look across every message.

![](/images/webflow/688c41e84826ae6871270c3e-680c5f92fee124cd0ee27842-67e66a5ef22bd710bdf6374e-group-2024.svg)

## AI content generation and import

Turn your product know-how into polished onboarding or training content with just a few clicks. Our new AI tools help you create and import lessons faster, so you can launch your academy in less time.
