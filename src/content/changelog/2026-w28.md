### New

- **Put your academy on your own domain.** Enter the address you want, copy the exact DNS records we show you, and activate it once your provider has picked them up. No support ticket, no waiting.
  _For example:_ your partner academy can live at `academy.yourcompany.com`, so partners never see a third-party address when they sign in.

- **Send academy emails from your own domain.** Verify your sending domain with the records we display, then choose the address invitations, certificates, and notifications go out from.
  _For example:_ a course invite arrives from `learning@yourcompany.com` instead of a generic sender, so it looks like you and lands in the inbox more often.

### Improved

- **Your homepage loads faster for first-time visitors.** The public entry page is now served from cache for anyone who isn't signed in, so an invited learner sees your academy almost instantly.
  _For example:_ send a cohort invite to 200 people and the landing page holds up even when they all click within the same minute.

### Fixed

- **Emails now link back to your own academy address.** Certificate and access-request emails previously pointed at a generic platform address. They now use the domain your academy actually runs on.
  _For example:_ a learner who earns a certificate gets a link on your domain that they can safely forward to their manager.
