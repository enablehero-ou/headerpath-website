### New

- **Upload your own videos straight into a course.** Drop an mp4, mov or webm file (up to 5 GB and 4 hours) into any step and it's ready to play for your learners, with no YouTube or Vimeo account needed. In a sequential course, learners must watch at least 90% of an uploaded video before they can continue.
  _For example:_ record a 20-minute product walkthrough for new hires, upload it to their first onboarding step, and know that nobody skips ahead to the quiz without watching it.

### Fixed

- **Dates and times now show correctly in your time zone.** Content lists, activity logs and certificates now display dates and relative times like "2 hours ago" in your local time, without flickering or showing the wrong value while the page loads.
  _For example:_ when you check who completed a compliance course this morning, the activity log shows the time as it was where you are, not in another time zone.
