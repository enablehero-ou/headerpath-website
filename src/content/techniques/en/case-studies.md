## What is a case study

A case study is a real-world example that shows how others have successfully solved a problem or achieved a goal using your product. It highlights the challenge, the solution, and the results—offering proof, context, and inspiration for new learners.

## Why it works

People learn best through relatable stories. Case studies offer social proof and practical clarity. They show not just *what* to do, but *how* it worked for others in a similar situation. This reduces uncertainty, builds trust, and helps learners see themselves in the success story.

## Where to use it

Use case studies when you need to shift mindsets, explain complex solutions, or build confidence in a process. They're effective after foundational learning, as reinforcement or motivation to move forward—especially when learners are hesitating to take action.

## Case studies for user onboarding and education

In user onboarding, case studies help new users understand how others achieved results quickly. For example, show how a peer company launched an integration in one week using your platform. It validates the effort and inspires action early in the journey.

## Case studies for customer education

In customer education, case studies can demonstrate advanced usage, process improvements, or strategic outcomes. For example, showcase how a retail brand improved inventory accuracy by using a feature set correctly—making the abstract more tangible.

## Case studies for partner enablement

For partners, case studies are powerful tools to demonstrate how others ramped up quickly, closed their first deal, or solved a complex client need using your platform. They provide playbooks and context that reduce time to first success and promote autonomy.

## Case studies for sales rep training

Sales reps benefit from case studies by learning how others handled objections, positioned the solution, or moved deals forward. This turns strategy into lived experience and sharpens their confidence when speaking to prospects in similar industries or use cases.
