## What is video tutorials

Video tutorials are short, guided videos that walk learners through a process, feature, or concept using visuals and narration. They simplify learning by showing exactly what to do, not just explaining it. These videos can be screen recordings, live demos, or animated walkthroughs, depending on the use case.

## Why it works

Video is a high-retention medium—people absorb more when they *see* and *hear* it. It reduces ambiguity, supports different learning styles, and gives learners confidence by making abstract concepts concrete. Plus, it's asynchronous and scalable, which is crucial for distributed teams or global customers.

## Where to use it

Use video tutorials wherever clarity and confidence matter: new feature rollouts, product walkthroughs, installation guides, compliance steps, or complex workflows. Especially powerful for visual or technical processes that would take too many words to explain.

## Video tutorials for user onboarding and education

Help new users get value faster by showing them exactly how to complete key tasks—logging in, integrating with other tools, setting up preferences. A short video reduces support tickets and builds early momentum. Bonus: users can pause, replay, or skip—perfect for varied learning speeds.

## Video tutorials for customer education

Use video to explain your product’s value in action. Tutorials can show how to solve real problems, demonstrate use cases, or highlight expert tips. Great for self-serve learning and reducing dependency on 1:1 support. Especially helpful in industries like manufacturing, software, or finance where the workflows are visual or regulated.

## Video tutorials for partner enablement

Your partners don’t have time to read 50-slide decks. Short videos that explain how to position, sell, or support your solution help them get ready faster. Use video to walk through sales playbooks, demo flows, or how to access partner tools. It's easy to localize and update when needed.

## Video tutorials for sales rep training

Whether it’s objection handling, demoing a new product line, or setting up internal tools—video shows the right way to do it. You can build a just-in-time library that reps can watch before calls, or embed short clips into learning paths to reinforce technique. And you can track completion for accountability.
