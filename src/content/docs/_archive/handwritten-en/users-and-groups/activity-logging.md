---
title: Activity Logging
description: Track every action taken in your account with the built-in activity log.
---

Qurioos keeps a complete audit trail of all admin and user activity. The log is available under **Admin → Logs** and is useful for understanding what happened, when, and who did it.

### What gets logged

The activity log captures 26 action types, including:

- **Authentication** — logins, logouts, signups
- **Invitations** — invites sent and accepted
- **Content** — pages and steps created, updated, published, unpublished, deleted
- **Users** — user updates and deletions
- **Groups** — groups created, updated, deleted
- **Certifications** — certificates earned
- **Settings** — account and settings updates
- **Translations** — translations created, auto-translated, published
- **Progress** — progress cleared

### How to filter the log

From **Admin → Logs**:

- Use the **date range** selector to narrow by time period
- Use the **user filter** to view activity from a specific person
- Entries are paginated — 20 per page

### How to read a log entry

Each entry shows the action type, the user who performed it, the affected resource (if any), and the timestamp.

**Need to know**

- The activity log is append-only and cannot be edited or deleted by admins.
- The recent activity feed on the admin dashboard shows the 10 most recent entries. Go to **Admin → Logs** for full history and filtering.
