---
title: Roles and Permissions
description: A detailed breakdown of all six roles available in your Qurioos account.
---

Every user in Qurioos has a role that determines what they can see and do. Roles apply per account — a user can have different roles in different academies.

### The six roles

**Superadmin**
Full platform access. Can do everything an Admin can do, plus use AI-assisted content generation. Typically reserved for the Qurioos platform team.

**Admin**
Full access to all admin features — content, users, groups, and settings. Cannot access AI generation. This is the appropriate role for your internal team's most trusted members.

**Manager**
Can manage users (Creators, Editors, and Users only — not Admins), manage groups, and access all content features. Cannot access Settings.

**Creator**
Can create, edit, and delete pages and steps. Cannot manage users or access Settings.

**Editor**
Can edit existing pages and steps only. Cannot create or delete content, manage users, or access Settings.

**User**
The user role. No admin panel access. Can view content, track progress, and earn certificates.

### Role hierarchy for user management

Managers can only manage users at or below their level. An Admin can manage all roles except Superadmin. A Manager can manage Creator, Editor, and User roles but not Admin or Superadmin.

### Group managers

Users with the **User** role can be designated as **Group Managers** for a specific group. Group managers get limited admin panel access (Users and Groups only) scoped to their assigned group(s). This is a separate concept from the Manager role.

**Need to know**

- You cannot assign a role higher than your own. An Admin cannot promote someone to Superadmin.
- Role changes take effect immediately on the user's next page load.
