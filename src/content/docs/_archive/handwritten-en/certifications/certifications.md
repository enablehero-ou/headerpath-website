title: Certifications
description: Award users a certificate when they complete a content page in your Qurioos account.

Certifications let you recognize users who complete a content page. Each page can have its own certification settings, including completion thresholds, expiry dates, and custom certificate designs.

### Understanding certifications in Qurioos

When a user meets the completion requirements of a certified page, Qurioos automatically generates a certificate with a unique credential ID. The certificate is accessible on the user's profile, can be downloaded as a PDF, and has a public URL they can share.

### How to enable certification for a page

1. Open the page in the content editor
2. Go to the **Certification** tab
3. Toggle **Enable certification** on
4. Configure the settings below

### Certification settings

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Completion threshold** | Minimum % of steps completed to qualify |
| **Quiz accuracy threshold** | Minimum % of quiz questions answered correctly |
| **Issuer name** | Name shown on the certificate (defaults to your account name) |
| **Credential prefix** | Prefix for the unique credential ID (e.g., `ACME-`) |
| **Certificate background** | Custom background image for the certificate PDF |
| **Expiry days** | Number of days until the certificate expires (leave blank for no expiry) |
| **Allow retry** | Whether users can retake the content page to recertify |
| **Max attempts** | Maximum number of certification attempts allowed |

### How users access their certificate

Users can view and download their certificates from their **Profile** page. Each certificate also has a public URL — for example, `yourcompany.com/certificate/[id]` — that users can share with employers or on LinkedIn.

**Need to know**

- Certifications are available on the **Starter** plan and above.
- Changing the completion threshold after users have already certified does not retroactively affect existing certificates.
