---
title: Branding
description: Customize your account's logo, favicon, colors, and typography.
---

Your branding settings control the visual identity of your account — what users see, not the admin panel. Configure everything under **Settings → Branding**.

### Logo

Upload your account logo. The logo appears in the user navigation bar and on the login page.

**Supported formats:** PNG, JPG — SVG is not supported.

### Favicon

Upload a favicon to display in browser tabs and bookmarks.

**Supported formats:** PNG, JPG — SVG is not supported. Use a square image, ideally 32×32px or 64×64px.

### Colors

| Setting | Where it appears |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Primary color** | Buttons, active states, links |
| **Accent color** | Highlights, badges, secondary elements |

Click the color swatch to open a color picker, or enter a hex value directly.

### Typography

Choose a font family for your account's user-facing UI. Available fonts:

*Assistant, Inter, Lato, Montserrat, Open Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Roboto*

The selected font applies to all text in the user UI, including page content.

### How to update branding

1. Go to **Settings → Branding**
2. Upload files or select values
3. Click **Save**

Changes are visible to users immediately.

**Need to know**

- SVG files are not accepted for logo or favicon uploads. Convert to PNG before uploading.
- Your logo is displayed at its natural aspect ratio. Use a transparent background PNG for best results on light and dark backgrounds.
