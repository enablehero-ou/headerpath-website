---
title: Homepage Customization
description: Configure the hero section, layout template, and featured content on your account homepage.
---

Your account homepage is fully configurable. Everything from the hero section to the page layout is controlled under **Settings → Homepage**.

### Hero section

The hero section is the first thing users see at the top of your homepage. Configure it with:

- **Title** — the main headline
- **Subtitle** — a short supporting line
- **CTA text and link** — a button label and destination URL
- **Hero image** — an uploaded image displayed in the hero area
- **Hero video** — a YouTube, Vimeo, or Streamable video URL (overrides the image if both are set)

### Templates

Choose how content is displayed below the hero:

| Template | Layout |
|----------|--------|
| **Grid** | All published pages displayed in a card grid |
| **Hero + Grid** | Featured pages highlighted at top, rest in a grid |
| **Featured + All** | Featured section followed by a full list |

### Featured sections

Toggle and title two optional sections:

- **Featured pages** — a curated selection of highlighted content pages
- **Featured steps** — individual steps promoted on the homepage

To mark a page as featured, open it in the editor and toggle **Featured** in the page settings.

### Localization

All homepage text fields (title, subtitle, CTA, section headings) support per-locale translations. If your account has multiple languages enabled, add translations under each locale tab in the Homepage settings.

**Need to know**

- Hero video takes priority over the hero image when both are set.
- If no pages are marked as featured, the Featured pages section shows empty when enabled.
