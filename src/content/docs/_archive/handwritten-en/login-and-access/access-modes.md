---
title: Access Modes
description: Control who can access your Qurioos account with three access mode options.
---

Your account's **access mode** determines who can sign up, sign in, and view content. You can change this at any time under **Settings → Access**.

### Login only (default)

Only users explicitly invited by an admin can access the account. Users who navigate to your account without an account see a login page but cannot create one themselves.

This is the most secure option and is recommended for internal training academies, employee onboarding, or any audience you want to control manually.

You can optionally enable **Request access** — a form that lets visitors request an account. Admins review and approve or deny requests from **Admin → Users → Access requests**.

### Public signup

Anyone can visit your account and create an account. Users sign up with their email and are immediately active — no admin approval needed.

Useful for open-enrollment academies, customer education, or community learning.

### Full public

No login is required at all. All published content is accessible to anyone with the link, including unauthenticated visitors. Progress is tracked via cookies for anonymous users.

Useful for marketing content, free content pages, or open reference material.

### How to change your access mode

1. Go to **Settings → Access**
2. Select the mode that fits your account
3. Click **Save**

**Need to know**

- Switching from **Full public** to **Login only** will immediately require authentication to view any content.
- Group-restricted pages are always protected, regardless of access mode.
