Qurioos is a multi-tenant learning platform. Each account gets its own branded environment with its own users, content, and settings. This guide is for admins, managers, creators, and editors — anyone who manages the account behind the scenes.

### Your admin panel

Log in to your account and click **Admin** in the navigation to access the admin panel. What you can do depends on your role:

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| **Superadmin** | Full platform access including AI content generation |
| **Admin** | Full access — manage all users, content, and settings |
| **Manager** | Manage creators, editors, and users; full content access |
| **Creator** | Create, edit, and delete content |
| **Editor** | Edit existing content only |
| **User** | Access content, no admin panel access |

### First steps

Start here to get your account up and running:

1. [Configure your settings](/articles/admin-guide/settings) — set your account name, logo, colors, and access mode
2. [Create your first content page](/articles/getting-started/first-course) — build and publish a Page with Steps
3. [Invite your team](/articles/getting-started/invite-users) — add admins, creators, and users
4. [Set up your homepage](/articles/admin-guide/homepage-customization) — customize the hero section and featured content

**Need to know**

- The admin panel is only available on desktop. User features are fully responsive.
- Your account URL is `yourslug.qurioos.com` by default. You can connect a custom domain under **Settings → Domain**.
- Questions? Email **support@qurioos.com**.
