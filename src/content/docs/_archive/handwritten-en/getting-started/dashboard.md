---
title: Admin Dashboard
description: An overview of the stats, quick actions, and activity feed on your admin dashboard.
---

The admin dashboard is the first screen you see when you enter the admin panel. It gives you a live snapshot of your account's activity and health.

### Stats cards

The top of the dashboard shows four key metrics, each with a 30-day trend indicator:

- **Total users** — all active members in your account
- **Total content** — published and draft pages combined
- **Completion rate** — the percentage of users who have completed at least one content page
- **Certified users** — the number of users who have earned at least one certificate (only shown if any content page has certification enabled)

### Quick actions

Below the stats, two shortcut buttons let you jump straight to the most common tasks:

- **Create content** — opens the new page form
- **Invite user** — opens the user invitation form

### Recent activity

The dashboard shows the 10 most recent entries from your activity log — logins, content changes, user invitations, and more. Click **View all activity** to see the full log with filtering options.

**Need to know**

- Stats are calculated in real time from your current data. The 30-day trend compares the current value to the value 30 days ago.
- The completion rate counts users who have completed at least one page, not the average across all pages.
- If you manage a large account, use **Admin → Logs** for more detailed activity filtering by user or date range.
