---
title: AI-Assisted Content Creation
description: Use AI to generate a complete content page structure from a short description.
---

Qurioos includes an AI content generation feature that can build out a full content page — pages, steps, quiz questions, and content — from a brief description of the topic and target audience. This is available to **Superadmin** users only.

### How it works

You describe the content page you want and the AI generates a complete draft: step structure, written content, quiz questions with correct answers, and callouts. You can then review and edit each step before publishing.

The AI uses your account's **brand voice** and **tone** settings (configured under **Settings → Account → About**) to match your organization's communication style.

### How to generate a content page

1. Go to **Admin → Content**
2. Click **New page**
3. Select **Generate with AI**
4. Enter a topic, target audience, and any specific requirements
5. Click **Generate**

Qurioos will process the request — this can take a minute or two. You'll see a generation status page. When complete, the draft page opens in the editor.

### After generation

Review every step before publishing. AI-generated content is a strong starting point but should always be reviewed for accuracy, tone, and fit with your specific context.

Edit steps the same way you would manually created content.

**Need to know**

- AI generation is available on Superadmin accounts only.
- Generation cost is tracked internally and contributes to account usage.
- Generated content is always created as a draft — it is never automatically published.
