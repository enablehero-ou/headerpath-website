---
title: Settings
description: Configure every aspect of your Qurioos account from the Settings panel.
---

The Settings panel has ten tabs. Go to **Admin → Settings** to access them.

### Homepage

Configure what users see on your account's homepage:

- **account name** — displayed in the browser tab and emails
- **Hero section** — title, subtitle, CTA button text and link, hero image or video (YouTube, Vimeo, or Streamable URL)
- **Template** — choose between *Grid*, *Hero + Grid*, or *Featured + All*
- **Featured pages section** — enable/disable and set a heading
- **Featured steps section** — enable/disable and set a heading

### Account

Business-level settings used as context for AI features and outbound emails:

- **About** — description, industry, tone, and brand voice
- **Company information** — name and address
- **Legal links** — privacy policy URL and terms of service URL
- **Reply-to email** — address used on all outbound emails

### Access

See [Access Modes](/articles/getting-started/access-modes) for a full explanation of Login only, Public signup, and Full public.

### Branding

- **Logo** — PNG or JPG only (SVG not supported)
- **Favicon** — PNG or JPG
- **Primary color** — main brand color
- **Accent color** — secondary highlight color
- **Font family** — choose from Assistant, Inter, Lato, Montserrat, Open Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, or Roboto

### Profile fields

Configure what information users provide at signup or on their profile page. System fields (first name, last name) can be set to required or optional. Custom fields support text, email, phone, URL, date, dropdown, and checkbox types.

### Navigation

Customize the user navigation bar. Add custom links and link groups (folders), toggle the content pages dropdown, and drag to reorder. Navigation items support per-locale translations.

### Domain

Connect a custom domain to your account. Your default URL is `yourslug.qurioos.com`. See [Custom Domain](/articles/admin-guide/custom-domain) for setup steps.

### Authentication

Toggle which login methods are available to users. **Magic link** (email) is always active. Enable or disable **Google** and **Microsoft** login.

### Privacy

- **Cookie consent banner** — enable to show a GDPR-compliant consent prompt before loading analytics
- **Custom consent script** — replace the built-in banner with your own CMP

### Plan

View your current plan (Free, Starter, Pro, or Enterprise) and usage against each limit: pages, users, admin users, languages, and groups. See [Plans and Limits](/articles/admin-guide/plans-limits) for a full breakdown.
