Qurioos offers four plans. Your current plan and usage are visible under **Settings → Plan**.

### Plan comparison

| Feature | Free | Starter | Pro | Enterprise |
|---------|------|---------|-----|-----------|
| **Pages** | 5 | 20 | 100 | Unlimited |
| **Users** | 25 | 100 | 1,000 | Unlimited |
| **Languages** | 1 | 3 | 12 | 12 |
| **Storage** | 100 MB | 500 MB | 5 GB | Unlimited |
| **Groups** | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Certifications** | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Analytics** | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **CSV export** | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Custom domain** | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Auto-translation** | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **API access** | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |

### Viewing your usage

Go to **Settings → Plan** to see a dashboard showing your current usage against each limit — pages, users, admin users, languages, and groups — displayed as progress bars.

### What happens when you hit a limit

Qurioos will prevent you from creating more of the limited resource (e.g., additional pages, additional users). Existing content and users are not affected. Upgrade your plan to increase the limit.

### Upgrading

Contact **support@qurioos.com** to upgrade your plan or discuss a custom Enterprise arrangement.

**Need to know**

- Limits count active records. Deleted pages and deactivated users do not count against your limits.
- Admin users (non-user roles) count separately from regular user limits on some plans.
