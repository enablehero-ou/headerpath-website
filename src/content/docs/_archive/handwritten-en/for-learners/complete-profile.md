---
title: Completing Your Profile
description: How to fill in your profile information when joining a Qurioos account.
---

When you join a Qurioos account for the first time — especially via Google or Microsoft login — you may be asked to complete your profile before accessing the account. This is a one-time step.

### Why this happens

Some academies require additional information (like your first name, last name, department, or employee ID) before you can access content. The profile completion screen collects this information at the start.

### How to complete your profile

1. Sign in using your email magic link or social login (Google or Microsoft)
2. If your profile is incomplete, you'll be taken to the **Complete your profile** screen automatically
3. Fill in all required fields
4. Click **Save and continue**

You'll be taken directly to the account after submitting.

### Updating your profile later

You can update your profile information at any time:

1. Click your name in the navigation bar
2. Select **Profile**
3. Edit your details and click **Save**

### What fields you'll see

The fields shown depend on what your account has configured. Common fields include:

- First name and last name
- Email address (usually pre-filled from your login)
- Department, job title, or employee ID (custom fields added by the admin)

**Need to know**

- Required fields (marked with an asterisk) must be filled in before you can proceed. Optional fields can be skipped.
- Your profile information is only visible to the admins of this account.
