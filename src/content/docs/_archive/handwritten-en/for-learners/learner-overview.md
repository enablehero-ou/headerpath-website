title: User Guide Overview
description: A guide for users on how to navigate and use your Qurioos account.
---

Welcome to your Qurioos account. This guide covers everything you need to find content pages, track your progress, and earn certificates.

### Signing in

Your account uses **passwordless login**. Enter your email address on the login page and check your inbox for a one-time sign-in link. Click the link to sign in — no password needed.

Your account may also offer **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft** as alternative options.

### Browsing content pages

After signing in, your account homepage shows available content pages. You can also browse all content pages by clicking **All content pages** in the navigation.

Each content page card shows:
- Content page title
- Your current progress (if you've started it)
- A certificate badge if the content page awards a certificate on completion

### Taking a content page

Click a content page to open it. Content pages are divided into **Steps** that you navigate one at a time using the **Continue** button or the sidebar menu.

- **Quiz steps** — answer the question before continuing. You must get it right to move forward.
- **Form steps** — submit a response (feedback, reflection, or file upload) to continue.
- **Podcast steps** — listen to the audio and follow along with the transcript.

Your progress is saved automatically.

### Your profile

Click your name in the navigation to visit your profile page. Here you can update your personal information and view all certificates you've earned.

**Need to know**

- If you can't sign in, check your spam folder for the magic link email or try signing in again.
- Progress is saved per device for visitors without an account. Sign in to keep your progress permanently.
