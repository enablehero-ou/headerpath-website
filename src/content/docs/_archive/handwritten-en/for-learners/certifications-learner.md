---
title: Earning and Sharing Your Certificate
description: How to earn, view, download, and share a certificate from a Qurioos content page.
---

Some content pages in your Qurioos account award a **certificate** when you meet the completion requirements. Your certificate is permanently stored on your profile and can be shared publicly.

### How to earn a certificate

To earn a certificate you must:

1. Complete the required percentage of steps in the content page
2. Meet the minimum quiz accuracy (if the content page has quiz steps)

The exact requirements vary per content page. When you meet them, your certificate is generated automatically — there's nothing extra to do.

### Where to find your certificates

Go to your **Profile** page (click your name in the navigation). All your earned certificates are listed there.

Click a certificate to open the full certificate view, where you can download it or copy the shareable link.

### Downloading your certificate

From the certificate view, click **Download PDF** to save a copy of your certificate to your device.

### Sharing your certificate

Each certificate has a **public URL** — a link that anyone can open to verify your certificate. You can share this link on LinkedIn, in your email signature, or with an employer.

The public URL stays valid as long as the certificate is active (not expired).

### Certificate expiry

Some content pages issue certificates with an expiry date. If your certificate expires, the public URL will show an expired state. Contact your account admin to discuss recertification.

**Need to know**

- Certificates are tied to your account. If you complete a content page anonymously (without signing in), you won't receive a certificate.
- The certificate displays your name as it appears on your profile. Update your profile before completing a content page if your name needs to be corrected.
