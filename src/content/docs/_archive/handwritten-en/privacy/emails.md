---
title: Emails
description: How Qurioos sends emails and how to configure your email settings.
---

Qurioos sends transactional emails on your behalf using **Resend**. All emails use your account name as the display name and a configurable reply-to address.

### Types of emails Qurioos sends

| Email | When it's sent |
|-------|---------------|
| **Magic link** | When a user requests a sign-in link |
| **Invitation** | When an admin invites a user |
| **Access request confirmation** | When a visitor submits a request to join |

### How to configure email settings

Go to **Settings → Account** to set:

- **Reply-to email** — the address users will reach when they reply to a Qurioos email. Defaults to a Qurioos address if not set.

The **from address** is configured at the platform level per account by the Qurioos team. Contact **support@qurioos.com** if you need a custom from address.

### Email delivery

Emails are sent from Qurioos infrastructure via Resend. Delivery depends on your users' email providers and spam filters. If users report not receiving emails:

- Ask them to check their spam or junk folder
- Ensure the from address is allowlisted by their IT department
- Contact **support@qurioos.com** if the issue persists

**Need to know**

- Qurioos does not currently support custom email templates. The design and copy of system emails are managed by the Qurioos team.
- Magic link emails contain a one-time sign-in link that expires after a short window.
