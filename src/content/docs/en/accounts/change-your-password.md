### Reset your account password

Both users and admins can reset their password on their own. If you’ve forgotten your Qurioos account password, you can easily reset it using the **Forgot password** link.

### How to reset your password

1. On the **Log in** page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address linked to your Qurioos account.
3. If an account with that email exists, you’ll receive an email with a password reset link.
4. Click the link in the email.
5. Create a new password and save your changes.

**Need to know**

- The reset link expires after a set time for security purposes. If it expires, repeat the steps above.
- If you don’t receive the reset email, check your spam or junk folder.

**Tip:** For a stronger password, use a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
