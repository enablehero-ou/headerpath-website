### Supported font types

Qurioos supports two types of fonts:

1. **Google Fonts**: Customize your account by choosing from over 900 free, open-licensed fonts provided by [Google Fonts ↗](https://fonts.google.com/).**‍**
2. **Imported Fonts**: If you have your own brand fonts, we can add them for you.

### Adding Google fonts

Google Fonts can be easily integrated into your account. Simply browse the available fonts and select the one that best matches your brand style.

### Importing fonts

To add your own fonts:

1. Email us at **support@qurioos.com** with your font files.
2. For maximum browser compatibility, send the fonts in the following formats:
   - `.ttf` (TrueType)
   - `.otf` (OpenType)
   - `.eot` (Embedded OpenType)
   - `.woff` (Web Open Font Format)

We’ll add the fonts to your account and let you know once they’re ready to use.

**Note:** Imported fonts is a premium feature. Check our [pricing page](/pricing) to learn more.
