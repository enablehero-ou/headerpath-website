### How to report abuse

If you believe a HeaderPath account contains prohibited content or is being used in a way that violates our [Services agreement](/legal/services-agreement), [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy), or [End user policy](/legal/end-user-policy) please let us know immediately.

All accounts hosted on HeaderPath must follow our terms and conditions, which outline the types of content and activities that are not allowed. This includes, but is not limited to, illegal content, harassment, spam, or other abusive behavior.

### How to submit a report

1. Gather any relevant details, such as:
   - The account URL
   - A description of the issue
   - Screenshots or other supporting evidence
2. Email **support@headerpath.com** with the subject line **Report abuse**.
3. Our team will review your report and take appropriate action.

**Need to know**

- Reports are confidential.
- We may reach out to you if we need more details to investigate.
