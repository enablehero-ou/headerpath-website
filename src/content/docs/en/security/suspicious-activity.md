### Report suspicious account activity

If you believe your Qurioos account has been hacked or accessed by someone else, contact **support@qurioos.com** immediately so we can investigate and confirm there are no issues.

### How to report

1. Gather any details you’ve noticed, such as unusual logins, changes you didn’t make, or unexpected emails.
2. Email **support@qurioos.com** with this information.
3. Our team will review your account activity, confirm whether there has been unauthorized access, and guide you through any necessary security steps.

**Need to know**

- Acting quickly reduces the risk of further unauthorized activity.
- We may temporarily lock your account while we investigate to keep your data secure.
