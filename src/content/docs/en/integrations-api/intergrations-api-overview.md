## How integrations work in HeaderPath

HeaderPath is designed to work alongside the tools you already use. Our platform can sync data both from your systems into HeaderPath (for things like user access) and from HeaderPath back to your systems (for reporting, progress, and certification updates). This means you don’t have to change how your team operates, HeaderPath fits right into your workflow.

## Typical use cases

Here are some common ways teams use our integrations and API:

- **Invite-only access**: Sync user lists or IDs from your HR, CRM, or identity system so that only the right people can join.
- **Learning progress tracking**: Send updates from HeaderPath back to your CRM, Slack, Teams, Airtable, or reporting tools so you always know who is progressing and completing.
- **Certification updates**: When a learner earns a certification, automatically log it in your CRM or internal dashboards.

## Systems we connect with

HeaderPath integrates with a wide variety of tools, from CRMs and HR platforms to communication apps and data tools. Examples include:

- CRMs like **Salesforce** or **HubSpot**
- Collaboration tools like **Slack** or **Microsoft Teams**
- Data managamenet and reporting tools like **Airtable** or **BI dashboards**, **HR** or **identity systems** for user management
