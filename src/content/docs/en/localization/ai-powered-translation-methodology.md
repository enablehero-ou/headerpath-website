### Proprietary AI translation system

Qurioos has developed a proprietary AI translation system specifically optimized for educational content. Unlike generic translation tools, our system understands:

- Educational terminology and pedagogy
- Assessment language and quiz structures
- Instructional design best practices
- Your company's unique context and terminology

### The 4-stage translation process

Qurioos uses a proprietary 4-stage AI system built specifically for educational content:

**Stage 1: Answer a few questions to build a glossary**

**‍**We ask you about your company, industry, product names, preferred terminology, and any cultural sensitivities. This ensures translations match your brand voice and context.

**Stage 2: AI localized translation**

**‍**Our education-trained AI model translates your entire course while applying your glossary, adapting cultural references, and maintaining instructional clarity.

**Stage 3: AI proofreading**

**‍**A specialized proofreading AI reviews the translation for grammar, cultural appropriateness, technical accuracy, and format integrity—then corrects any issues automatically.

**Stage 4: Quality report & review**

**‍**You receive an email with a quality score, summary of corrections made, and a link to review the final translation before publishing.

**Result:**

Professional-quality translations in minutes, not weeks—with your company's unique context baked in.
