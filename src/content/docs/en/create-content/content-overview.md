### Understanding the HeaderPath content structure

HeaderPath organizes all learning experiences with a simple, flexible hierarchy. This structure works for anything — from a single course to a multi-track certification program — all within one account.

### **Account**

Your HeaderPath account contains all of your content.

### **Subjects**

A **Subject** is the top-level container for a learning experience. Examples:

- *Learn Stripe* (product education)
- *Atlassian Partner Program* (partner certification)
- *Introduction to Sales* (general skills course)

### **Levels**

Each Subject can have zero or more **Levels** that organize content.

- Hide Levels if you don’t need them.
- Use Levels if your learning experience has tiers or progression.

Examples:

- *Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced* (by mastery level)
- *Platinum / Gold / Silver* partner tiers (by milestones)

### **Paths**

Each Level can have zero or more **Paths**.

- Hide Paths if you don’t need them.
- Use Paths when you want to break a Level into role- or focus-based tracks.

Examples:

- *Stripe for Designers / Stripe for Marketeers / Stripe for Developers* (by role)
- *Sales for B2B / Sales for B2C / Sales for B2G* (by type of activity)

### **Modules**

Paths contain **Modules**, which group related topics together.

Examples:

- *Getting started with Stripe front end*
- *UI/UX customizations*
- *Role play* or *Use cases* in a sales course

### **Steps**

Steps are the individual content pages learners interact with. They are designed for **microlearning** — short, focused learning moments.

A Step can include:

- Text
- Images
- Videos
- Quizzes or assessments
- Other interactive elements

### Examples in practice

**Example 1 – Product learning**

- **Subject:** *Learn Stripe*
- **Levels:** None (hidden)
- **Paths:** *Stripe for Designers*, *Stripe for Developers*, *Stripe for Marketeers*
- **Example Modules** under *Stripe for Designers*: *Getting started with Stripe as a Designer*, *UI/UX customizations*
- **Steps:** Short, interactive lessons with text, media, and quizzes

**Example 2 – Partner certification**

- **Subject:** *Atlassian Partner Program*
- **Levels:** *Platinum Marketplace Partner*, *Gold Marketplace Partner*, *Silver Marketplace Partner* (each with specific benefits)
- **Example Paths** under *Silver Marketplace Partner*: *Getting started for Silver status*, *Verification steps*, *Technical requirements*
- **Example Modules** under *Getting started for Silver status*: *What you need to get started*, *Setting up your account*
- **Steps:** Short, interactive lessons with text, media, and quizzes

**Example 3 – General skills course**

- **Subject:** *Introduction to Sales*
- **Levels:** *Beginner*, *Intermediate*, *Advanced*
- **Example Paths** under *Beginner*: *Beginner Sales for B2B*, *Beginner Sales for B2C*
- **Example Modules** under *Beginner Sales for B2B*: *Getting started with B2B Sales*, *What is a lead*, *Best ways to use a CRM***‍**
- **Steps:** Short, interactive lessons with text, media, and quizzes
