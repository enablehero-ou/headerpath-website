### Overview

Qurioos allows users to add earned certifications directly to their LinkedIn profiles. This highlights skills publicly while linking the achievement to the organization that issued it.

### How it works

1. Users click the **“Add to LinkedIn”** button on the awarded certification.
2. A **pre-filled certification form** opens in the user’s LinkedIn account.
3. Users review and confirm the form details.
4. The certification appears on the user’s LinkedIn profile, connected to the **Qurioos account owner company**.

### Example

If **ACME Corp.** owns a Qurioos account, all certifications from their learning experiences will list **ACME Corp.** as the issuing organization on LinkedIn.
