### Understanding certifications in HeaderPath

Certifications in HeaderPath are fully customizable credentials awarded to users when they meet specific criteria. They are always tied to a **Level**, which is connected to a **Subject**, and an unlimited number of certifications can exist in an account.

### Awarding certifications

Certification criteria can be based on one or both of the following:

- **Progress %** — For example, award a certificate only when a user has completed **100%** of all Steps, Modules, and Paths in a Level.
- **Correct answers %** — For example, require users to score **at least 80%** across all Quiz Steps in the Level before awarding the certificate.

### Formatting and customization

Every certificate can be:

- Fully branded with custom **colors**, **logos**, and **texts**.
- Generated as a **PDF**.
- Issued with a **unique ID** for verification.
- Downloaded instantly upon completion.
