### Restart progress and try again from scratch

When a certification is linked to a Level, users may be allowed to retake it. Retaking a Level clears all progress for that Level, allowing the user to start fresh. This is useful if the user didn’t achieve the desired results and wants to improve their performance.

### How it works

- A **Restart/Retake** button appears for eligible levels.
- Clicking the button **clears all progress** in that level, including step completions and quiz results.
- The user can go through the level again from the beginning, completing all steps, quizzes, and challenges.
- Once they meet the certification requirements, the certification is awarded (or re-awarded).

### Example

A user is working on the **Platinum Partner** level, which requires 100% completion and at least 85% correct quiz answers. They complete the level but only score 70% on quizzes. By using the **Restart** button, their progress is reset to 0% and they can reattempt the entire level to meet the requirement.

### **Note for admins**‍

Only levels attached to a certification can be retaken. The retake option is enabled or disabled in the level’s settings by an admin.
