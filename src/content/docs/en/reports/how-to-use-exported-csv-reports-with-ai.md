### **Using CSV reports with AI for custom insights**‍

Dashboards are great for quick overviews, they show trends, charts, and key numbers. But they’re fixed. If the exact question isn’t built into the dashboard, the answer might require guesswork or manual calculations.

By [exporting your progress data as a CSV](/docs/reports-overview) and uploading it to a Large Language Model (LLM) like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, open source LLMs or the ones approved by your company, it’s possible to ask *any* question and get a direct answer.

### Why this approach works

- **Unlimited questions**: Ask exactly what’s needed instead of relying on preset filters.
- **Context-aware analysis**: LLMs can connect different pieces of data in the CSV to provide deeper insights.
- **Faster answers**: Skip building complex dashboard queries or waiting for a report from a data team.

### Examples of questions an LLM can answer from the CSV

**Overview insights**

- Which Level had the lowest quiz pass rate in Q1 2025?
- How many users completed *any* Subject in the last 7 days?
- What percentage of users got certified in multiple Levels this year?

**User-specific insights**

- Which users started but didn’t finish Module “UI/UX Customizations”?
- Show me all users who passed every quiz but didn’t complete all Steps in their Path.

**Comparisons and trends**

- Compare completion rates between “Beginner” and “Advanced” Levels for the last quarter.
- Identify the Subjects with the biggest drop in engagement after the first Step.
