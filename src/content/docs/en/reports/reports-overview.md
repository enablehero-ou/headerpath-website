### Exporting and using progress reports

Progress reports in HeaderPath give a detailed view of how users are engaging with content, from Subjects down to individual Modules. These reports are designed for both quick analysis and AI-assisted insights.

### How to export a progress report

1. Go to **Users** in the navigation menu.
2. Click **Export progress reports**.
3. A CSV file will be downloaded with user and progress data.

### What’s included in the CSV

Each row contains information for one user, including:

- **User ID**
- **First Name**
- **Last Name**
- **Email**
- **Role** (e.g., Admin, User)
- **Status** (e.g., Active, Inactive)
- **Created At** (account creation date)
- **Subject(s) Progress** and **Correct Answers %**
- **Level(s) Progress** and **Correct Answers %**
- **Path(s) Progress** and **Correct Answers %**
- **Module(s) Progress** and **Correct Answers %**

This structure repeats for each Subject in the content hierarchy (Level, Path, Module).

### Optimized for AI tools

The exported CSV is formatted so it can be uploaded to most large language models (LLMs) for analysis. Learn how to get the exact answers you need from the CSV.
