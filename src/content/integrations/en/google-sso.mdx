## Overview

The Google SSO integration lets learners access your academy using their existing Google Workspace or personal Google account. One click — no separate username or password to remember.

## What it enables

- **Zero-friction access** — learners click "Sign in with Google" and land directly on their dashboard
- **Automatic provisioning** — new learners are created in your academy when they first log in
- **Domain restriction** — optionally restrict access to users from your company domain (e.g. only `@yourcompany.com`)
- **Consistent identity** — learner profiles are linked to their Google account, so they're always the same person regardless of device

## Setup

1. In your academy settings, go to **Authentication → Single Sign-On**
2. Enable Google as an identity provider
3. Optionally restrict to specific domains
4. Share the academy URL with your learners — they'll see the Google sign-in button automatically

## Who it's for

Best for organizations where employees or partners already use Google Workspace. It removes the single biggest friction point in learner adoption: creating yet another account.
