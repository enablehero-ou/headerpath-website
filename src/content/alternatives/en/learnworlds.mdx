## The core difference

LearnWorlds is built for course creators — individuals and small teams who want to publish and sell online courses. It's a good product for that use case.

We're built for organizations — companies that need to run internal training, partner enablement, or customer education programs. These are fundamentally different problems.

## Who should choose LearnWorlds

- Individual course creators monetizing knowledge
- Solopreneurs building a course business
- Organizations that just need a content library and have internal L&D to design it

## Who should choose us

- Organizations running employee onboarding or training
- Companies building partner or channel enablement programs
- Businesses that need customer education tightly integrated with their product journey
- Teams that want design support, not just a platform to upload content
